How to use without touching the phone after placement
1. Print NEW V4.3 REF / LEFT / RIGHT full-size A4 targets at 100% (older prints are not calibrated for this geometry). 2. Put phones on a stable bar, and open this page on each phone. 3. Pair using the 6-digit code. 4. Enable the phone gravity sensor where supported. 5. Point both cameras at REF in turn and capture it automatically after steady tracking; the reference must remain physically fixed. 6. Keep phones fixed and move the LEFT / RIGHT targets into view. 7. Select the front or rear axle using the host phone. 8. If a wrong target is detected, the border turns red and tracking is blocked. 9. Measure rear by moving target plates, not the stand, if possible. Do not use these readings for safety-critical adjustments until independently verified.
Vertical indicator and lighting
The app uses the phone's own motion sensor to estimate gravity. “Target upright” checks rotation within the board plane; “Vertical tilt” estimates the board-plane inclination and is not factory camber without wheel mounting compensation. If gravity cannot be read, both values say UNAVAILABLE; the program does not guess. Use diffuse white light; consumer phone cameras often block infrared.
Anti-jitter and flicker protection
The app checks target identity, at least 6 valid markers and reprojection error, then applies a Hampel/MAD outlier gate, a constant-velocity Kalman estimator, and adaptive One-Euro smoothing. It monitors angle scatter and ambient light flicker. If the measurement suddenly jumps, it displays UNSTABLE and hides the angle rather than presenting it as a real adjustment. Steady readings resume automatically after sufficient consistent frames. These mathematical methods improve repeatability but cannot guarantee accuracy or correct wheel mounting errors.
Camera calibration, reference and vehicle settings
The software estimates a planar target pose from 12 square coded markers and a known A4 layout. Approximate camera focal length is used until multiple varied REF views provide a plausible intrinsic calibration. Calibration and target identity have separate checks. Vehicle model and year are saved for documentation, not manufacturer reference geometry. Wheel-plane mounting error, wheel runout and thrust-line alignment still require physical compensation; do not assume absolute toe or camber is accurate without validation.
